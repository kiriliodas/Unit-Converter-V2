## What's new

- Added **Report a problem**, available from the flag button beside Settings on the Convert screen and from **Settings > Support**.
- The in-app form collects a title, description, optional reproduction steps, and up to five optional attachments such as screenshots, videos, or logs.
- Tapping **Open email app** prepares an email with the report and minimal technical details (app version, Android version, and device model). You review everything in your email app before sending.
- Conversions, favorites, and history are not included automatically. Nothing is sent by Unit Converter itself.

## Compatibility

- Android 8.0 (API 26) or later
- An installed email app is required to send a prepared report
- Application ID: `com.kirilliodas.unitconverter`
- Version: `2.1.2` (versionCode `4`)

Download `Unit-Converter-V2.1.2.apk`. The matching `Unit-Converter-V2.1.2.apk.sha256` file is provided for integrity verification.
