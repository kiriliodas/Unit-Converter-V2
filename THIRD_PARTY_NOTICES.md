## Google Material Symbols Rounded

The vector drawables whose names begin with `ic_symbol_` in
`app/src/main/res/drawable/` are local Android vector exports of Google
Material Symbols Rounded glyphs, converted from the upstream SVG path
data without changing the glyph geometry.

generated Material icons-extended artifact.
